About Dr. Agrawal’s R.K. Hospital

Dr. Agrawal’s R.K. Hospital is a trusted Orthopedic and Gynecology hospital in Nagpur, located at 27, Chandrashekhar Azad Square. We are known for Robotic Knee Replacement, fracture and trauma care, sports injuries, and spine treatment.

Our women’s care team offers pregnancy monitoring, normal and cesarean delivery, infertility support, menstrual and hormonal treatments, and gynecologic surgery.

With 24/7 emergency care, ICU, digital X-ray and ultrasound facilities, we provide safe and personalized treatment to patients across Nagpur and Central India.

Dr. Priyanka Jain Agrawal

Obstetrician & Gynecologist

Dr. Priyanka Jain Agrawal is a trusted Obstetrician-Gynecologist in Nagpur, specializing in pregnancy care, infertility treatment, and women’s health. She offers antenatal checkups, normal and cesarean deliveries, menstrual and hormonal disorder management, and advanced laparoscopic and hysteroscopy procedures.

Known for her patient-friendly approach, clear guidance, and ethical treatment, Dr. Priyanka supports women through every stage—from planning a pregnancy to childbirth and postpartum care. She practices at Dr. Agrawal’s R.K. Hospital, Central Avenue, Nagpur.

Dr. Rahul R. Agrawal

Orthopedic Surgeon

Dr. Rahul R. Agrawal is a trusted Orthopedic Surgeon in Nagpur, known for treating fractures, joint problems, and sports injuries with a patient-first approach. He specializes in joint replacement, robotic-assisted knee and hip surgery, spine treatment, trauma care, arthroscopy, deformity correction, and pediatric orthopedics.
