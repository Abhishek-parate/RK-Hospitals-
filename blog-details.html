Joint Replacement Surgery: What to Expect Before & After

  • Orthopedics
  • 10 Jan 2026
  • Dr. R.K. Agrawal
18 142

Joint replacement surgery is one of the most successful and life-changing orthopedic procedures available today. Whether it is a knee replacement or a hip replacement, the goal is the same — to relieve chronic pain, restore mobility, and help patients return to a comfortable, active life. At Dr. Agrawal's R.K. Hospital, Nagpur, our orthopedic team performs hundreds of joint replacement surgeries each year with excellent outcomes and high patient satisfaction.

Who Needs Joint Replacement Surgery?

Joint replacement is typically recommended when conservative treatments such as medications, physiotherapy, and lifestyle modifications no longer provide adequate relief. Common candidates include patients suffering from severe osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, avascular necrosis, or post-traumatic arthritis. If you experience persistent joint pain that interferes with daily activities like walking, climbing stairs, or even resting, it may be time to consult an orthopedic specialist.

Before the Surgery — Preparation is Key

A thorough pre-operative evaluation is carried out before scheduling your surgery. This includes blood tests, X-rays, ECG, and a fitness assessment to ensure you are in the best possible condition for the procedure. Our team will guide you through any necessary lifestyle changes — including weight management, stopping certain medications, and pre-operative physiotherapy exercises — that can significantly improve your surgical outcome and speed up recovery.

You will also be counselled about the type of implant being used. At R.K. Hospital, we use internationally certified implants from trusted manufacturers that are designed for longevity and natural joint movement. Our team will walk you through the entire process, answer your questions, and make sure you feel confident and informed before you go into the operation theatre.

The Surgical Procedure

Joint replacement surgery is typically performed under spinal or general anaesthesia and takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. During the procedure, the damaged bone and cartilage are carefully removed and replaced with a prosthetic implant made of metal alloy, high-grade plastic, or ceramic components. Our surgeons use minimally invasive techniques wherever possible to reduce tissue damage, minimize blood loss, and promote faster healing.

After the Surgery — Recovery & Rehabilitation

Recovery begins almost immediately. In most cases, patients are encouraged to stand and take their first steps with the help of a physiotherapist within 24 hours of surgery. This early mobilization plays a crucial role in preventing blood clots and stiffness. A dedicated rehabilitation programme is designed for each patient, covering strengthening exercises, gait training, and gradual return to daily activities.

Most patients are discharged within 3 to 5 days and can resume light activities within 4 to 6 weeks. Full recovery, including return to regular walking without support, is typically achieved within 3 months. With the right implant and proper physiotherapy, a well-done joint replacement can last 15 to 20 years or more. Our team at R.K. Hospital remains with you every step of the way — from your first consultation to your long-term follow-up care.
