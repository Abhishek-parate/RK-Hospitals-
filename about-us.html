About RK Hospital

Providing Trusted Healthcare Services in Nagpur

"Dr. Agrawal’s R.K. Hospital is a trusted Orthopedic and Gynecology hospital in Nagpur, located at 27, Chandrashekhar Azad Square. We are known for Robotic Knee Replacement, fracture and trauma care, sports injuries, and spine treatment. Our women’s care team offers pregnancy monitoring, normal and cesarean delivery, infertility support, menstrual and hormonal treatments, and gynecologic surgery. With 24/7 emergency care, ICU, digital X-ray and ultrasound facilities, we provide safe and personalized treatment to patients across Nagpur and Central India."
