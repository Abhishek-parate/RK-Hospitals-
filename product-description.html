health needs of adolescents through age-appropriate
medical guidance and evaluation.
Available with access from Seminary Hills.

transmitted infections through confidential screening.
Available at Dr. Agrawal's R.K. Hospital
with access from Medical Square.

through accurate and timely medical testing.
Available at Dr. Agrawal's R.K. Hospital

Obstetrics

childbirth and post-delivery monitoring with
medical supervision at every stage.

Infertility Testing & Treatment

Infertility testing and treatment focus on identifying
causes of delayed conception and providing
proper evaluation and care.
Access from Civil Lines.
